How ScanFlow AI works

A simple process designed to reduce confusion before the patient reaches the radiology department.

1

Receive Your Link

After booking an appointment, the patient receives a ScanFlow AI link or QR code.

2

Prepare at Home

Patients answer a short questionnaire and receive personalised preparation information.

3

Digital Check-In

Patients scan their QR code when they arrive at the hospital.

4

Staff Dashboard

Radiographers can quickly see preparation status and important patient notes.
